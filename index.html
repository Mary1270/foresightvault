Reputable source allowlist

Only these domains ever count as corroborating evidence (REPUTABLE_NEWS_DOMAINS):

reuters.com · apnews.com · bbc.com · npr.org · theguardian.com · nytimes.com · bloomberg.com · cnbc.com · aljazeera.com · politico.com · axios.com · cnn.com · washingtonpost.com · wsj.com

Stake GEN

Resolve

Opens at the resolution deadline and stays open for 48 hours. Submit https article URLs (one per line, one per outlet) that report the confirmed outcome: 2–6 on the first attempt, then only new outlets not under "Recorded sources", up to 6 recorded in total. Recorded evidence is final, and every required domain must be covered. Validators re-check every source independently; polls, projections and pages that don't literally state the outcome are ignored.

Expire

If the resolution window closes without a decisive result, anyone can switch the market to refunds. Every staker then claims back exactly what they staked.

Withdraw

Sends your entire credited balance (from claims) to your connected wallet. Claim each settled market from its own page first.
